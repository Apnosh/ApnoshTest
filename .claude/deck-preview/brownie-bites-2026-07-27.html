02 · Creative direction

On the table

Ideas from the email, and what we think of each.

Good forWatch outWe say
A photoshootTwo part flat lay. Stacked brownies, plus one brownie with raw ingredients.
A polished, professional look. That is worth paying for when you are reaching a big audience and the brand has to carry weight on its own.
Expensive, and slow to organize. For one local shop the payoff is small. Other things move the needle far more right now.
Skip it for now. Put the money and the hours where they show up faster. Revisit it once there is an audience big enough to justify the polish.
Chasing trendsTikTok trends, a dot brownie, seasonal and holiday stunts.
Cheap reach when you catch one. Seasonal stunts hit hard, but only with people who already follow along.
Hard to coordinate, and they move fast. A trend planned for next week is already dead. Seasonal posts get lost without an audience watching for the next flavor.
Do not build the plan around trends. If one fits, shoot it that day, keep it rough, and put it straight to stories. Build the habit first: a steady flavor update so people learn there is always something new. That is what makes the seasonal stunts land later.

03 · Where we stand

Three things worth flagging.

01

Late night is real. Is it visible?

  • Their own site says the shop is open until 11 PM Friday and Saturday. The delivery listing we checked showed ordering stopping around 8:40 PM. If the platforms disagree, they lose the exact hours that make them different.
  • Check Google and every delivery app against the real hours.
02

Price and portion keep coming up

  • Reviews say a brownie felt like five dollars for two or three bites, and that forty dollars for four was not worth the size. The questionnaire says their customer does not care much about price. The reviews disagree.
  • This is a menu, portion and packaging conversation. Not a branding one.
03

Consistency complaints exist

  • Wrong items, a dry mint brownie, food arriving cold. Some of that is the delivery platform, not the shop.
  • Spending on marketing before this is fixed buys more reviews, good and bad.
03 · Where we stand

Branding is not wasted.
It is early.

Branding multiplies distribution. Their distribution is close to zero right now, and anything multiplied by close to zero stays close to zero.

Build the traffic first. The brand work is what makes the second visit happen. It is phase two, not cut.
