Why 500 is the magic number

Most local businesses either give up before 100 followers or waste months chasing numbers that don't pay rent. 500 local followers changes the math. At 500 engaged local followers, Instagram and Facebook algorithms start showing your posts to friends-of-followers in your area. You start getting DMs. People recognize your name before they walk in. The first 500 is harder than the next 5,000 — but it unlocks everything else.

The system below has one rule: every follower should be a potential customer within 10 miles of your door. 50 local followers who care beat 5,000 bots who don't.

The 3 content pillars (and the ratio that works)

Post without a formula and you'll burn out or go silent. Local businesses that grow consistently use three types of content in a specific ratio:

👀
Show the work
50%
Behind-the-scenes, process shots, team moments. People buy from people they know.
💡
Teach something
30%
Tips, how-tos, "did you know" content in your category. Saves get you reach.
🎯
Promote
20%
Deals, events, new products. Never lead with this — earn attention first.

The 90-day phase plan

Don't try to do everything at once. This is a three-month build with a clear focus each month:

1
Days 1–30 · Foundation
0 → 150 followers

Post 4 times per week. Introduce yourself and your team, show your space, and share your story. Follow every local business, local influencer, and neighborhood account you can find. Like and comment on their posts — genuinely, not with spam. DM new followers with a simple welcome. Don't promote anything yet.

Week 1 move: Reintroduce your business as if it's new. Pin that post to the top of your profile.
2
Days 31–60 · Consistency
150 → 350 followers

Move to 5 posts per week. Introduce a recurring format — a weekly tip, a "meet the team" Thursday, a transformation Tuesday. Recurring formats train your audience to look for you. Start using local hashtags (your city + neighborhood + business type). Run one collaboration with a neighboring business — tag each other, do a giveaway, or go live together.

Collaboration hack: Tag a complementary business in a post ("our customers love pairing us with..."). They often reshare it.
3
Days 61–90 · Momentum
350 → 500+ followers

Add one Reel or short video per week — local algorithms heavily favor video right now. Create a "locals love" moment: ask followers to share your post for a small discount or entry into a giveaway. Post an offer exclusive to social followers. By now, your regular commenters are your real community — feature them (with permission). They'll share that post to everyone they know.

Milestone post: When you hit 400 followers, post a "thank you + what's coming" teaser. Appreciation posts always spike engagement.

Which platform to start on

Pick one platform and do it well. Most local businesses spread too thin. Here's how to choose:

Platform Best for Local reach Start here if...
Instagram Visual businesses, food, retail High Your product photographs well
Facebook 35+ audience, events, community High Your customers are 35+
TikTok Younger crowd, restaurants, salons Med You're comfortable on video
Nextdoor Services, home improvement, health Very high You serve people within 5 miles
LinkedIn B2B, professional services Low Your customers are other businesses

What to post when you have nothing

Every owner hits a "content blank" moment. Here's a week of posts that require no planning, no photoshoot, and no copywriter:

Monday
Show your Monday prep. Unlocking the door, stocking shelves, writing the specials. Caption: "Another week, another chance to..."
Tuesday
A tip from your category. "3 things to look for in a good [your product]." You already know this — write it like you're texting a friend.
Wednesday
Repost a customer review (with a thank you). Tag the neighborhood if you know it. Add "This made our week ❤️"
Thursday
Meet the team. One sentence bio per person. Include what they order/make/use most. Customers love knowing who makes their experience.
Friday
Your weekend special or hours. Keep it simple: "We're open [hours]. Come see us." + a photo of your most popular item.

The one thing that kills local growth fastest

Buying followers. Seriously — don't. Instagram and Facebook's algorithms detect fake engagement and quietly suppress your account. You'll get 200 followers who never interact, which tanks your engagement rate, which means the algorithm shows your posts to fewer real people. It takes months to recover.

The second-fastest killer: going silent for more than 2 weeks. Algorithms treat inactive accounts like ghost towns. If you need to take a break, schedule a few posts in advance using Meta Business Suite (it's free). Even one post per week keeps your account warm.
