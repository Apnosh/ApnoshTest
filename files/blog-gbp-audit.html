The Google Business Profile audit every local business needs to do today

Most GBP profiles are leaving money on the table. This 15-minute audit covers the 8 fields that actually affect your Google Maps ranking — with a clickable checklist you can work through right now.

⭐
Apnosh TeamLocal SEO
·June 10, 2026·5 min read
⭐

Google says fully completed Business Profiles get 7x more clicks than incomplete ones. In practice, we find that most local businesses have completed about 60% of their profile — leaving the remaining 40% to eat into their rankings and click rates.

This audit is designed to take 15 minutes. Open your Google Business Profile Manager in a new tab and work through these 8 items. Check each one off as you go — the checkboxes below actually work.

How to access your GBP: Search for your business name on Google. If you’re logged in as the owner, you’ll see an "Edit profile" button. Click that, or go directly to business.google.com.

The 8-point GBP audit checklist

Business name is exactly correct High impact
Matches your signage and legal name. No keyword stuffing (e.g., "Joe's Pizza — Best Pizza in Austin" is against Google's TOS and can get your listing suspended).
Primary category is set and specific High impact
"Pizza restaurant" beats "restaurant." Check what your top competitors are using — your primary category should match the most specific accurate descriptor for what you do.
Secondary categories are filled in High impact
Add every relevant secondary category. If you're a restaurant that also does catering, add "Caterer." If you're a salon that does both hair and nails, both categories should be present.
Business description uses real keywords Medium impact
Your 750-character description should naturally mention your city, neighborhood, services, and 2–3 key differentiators. Write for humans first — no keyword lists. Example: "Austin's most-reviewed Korean BBQ restaurant in the Domain area, open until midnight on weekends."
Hours are accurate (including special hours) High impact
Inaccurate hours are the #1 cause of 1-star reviews ("We drove 20 minutes and they were closed"). Update holiday hours proactively. Google lets you set special hours weeks in advance.
Phone number matches your website exactly High impact
Name, Address, Phone (NAP) consistency is a ranking signal. Your GBP phone must match your website, Yelp, and every other directory. Even minor formatting differences matter.
At least 20 real photos are uploaded High impact
Listings with 100+ photos get 520% more calls. Start with 20: your exterior, interior, best products/dishes, and team. Add new photos monthly. Owner-uploaded photos outrank customer-uploaded photos in prominence.
A GBP post was published in the last 7 days Medium impact
Posts signal activity to Google. A weekly post (an update, offer, event, or new product) keeps your profile fresh. Posts expire after 7 days for offers, 6 months for standard posts. Make it a weekly habit.

After the audit: the next step

If you checked all 8, your profile is in solid shape. The next focus is review velocity — getting a consistent flow of new reviews every month, not one big push. See our review strategy article for the system we use with clients.

If you had more than 2 unchecked items, those are your priority. Each one is potentially costing you ranking positions and clicks from customers who are actively looking for what you offer right now.
